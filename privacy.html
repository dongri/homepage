Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dongri Jin ("I", "me") handles your information when you use the websites and personal projects I operate (the "Services").

Information I receive

Some Services let you sign in with an account from a third-party sign-in provider. When you do, the provider shares the following with the Services, with your consent:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile picture
  • A unique identifier for your account with that provider

The Services never receive your password and do not request access to your email, files, calendar, contacts, or any other data held by the provider.

Like most websites, the Services may also record basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, and access times in server logs.

How I use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in
  • To create and identify your account in a Service
  • To show your name and profile picture inside a Service
  • To contact you about your account when necessary
  • To keep the Services secure and prevent abuse

I do not use your information for advertising, and I do not send marketing email.

Sharing

I do not sell, rent, or trade your information. It is shared only with the infrastructure providers that host the Services and store their data, and only as needed to run them, or when disclosure is required by law.

Cookies

The Services use cookies or similar browser storage only to keep you signed in and remember your settings. They are not used for cross-site tracking or advertising.

Retention and deletion

Your information is kept for as long as your account in a Service exists. You can ask me to delete your account and the data associated with it at any time by emailing the address below, and I will delete it within 30 days.

You can also revoke the Services' access at any time from the account settings of your sign-in provider.

Security

The Services are served over HTTPS and access to stored data is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
